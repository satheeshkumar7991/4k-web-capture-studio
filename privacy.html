Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

This policy explains what data the 4K Web Capture Studio Chrome extension ("the extension", "we") handles and why. The extension is operated by Satheeshkumar A, India.

Screenshots, videos and web pages

Screenshots and videos you capture, and the pages you capture them from, are processed entirely inside your browser. They are stored locally in your browser (IndexedDB) and saved to your computer only when you download them. We never upload, collect, view or share your captures, the content of the pages you visit, or your browsing history.

Data we handle

  • Email address. You enter it to start the free trial. It is stored locally in your browser. When you choose to subscribe, the extension opens the Dodo Payments checkout with your email filled in, so your purchase is linked to it.
  • License key. After you subscribe, Dodo Payments issues a license key. The extension sends this key (and a device activation ID and the device name "4K Web Capture Studio (your platform)") to Dodo Payments to activate it and, about once a day, to check that your subscription is still active.
  • Settings and trial status. Stored locally in your browser only.

Payments

Payments are processed by Dodo Payments, our merchant of record. Your card, UPI or other payment details are entered on Dodo's checkout page and are never seen or stored by the extension or by us. Dodo Payments handles that data under its own privacy policy. We may see your name, email, country and purchase history in our Dodo dashboard so we can provide support and manage your subscription.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell or transfer user data to third parties, except to Dodo Payments for the payment and license purposes above.
  • We do not use data for advertising, profiling, creditworthiness or lending.
  • We do not use analytics or tracking in the extension.

Keeping and deleting data

Everything stored by the extension lives in your browser. Removing the extension deletes it. Using "Remove from this browser" in the Subscription tab frees the license on that device. To ask us to delete your purchase records held by Dodo Payments (subject to tax and legal record-keeping requirements), email us.

Children

The extension is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the "Last updated" date.
