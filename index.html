4K Web Capture Studio

A Chrome extension that captures any website as a full-page screenshot or a smooth auto-scrolling video, with built-in image and video editors. Your captures stay on your device.

Add to Chrome

Features

Full-page screenshots

Scrolls the page, loads lazy images and stitches one clean image without repeating sticky headers.

Auto-scroll video

Records the tab while it scrolls, waiting for on-screen animations and videos to finish.

Image & video editor

Crop, draw, add text, blur sensitive areas; trim, cut and change video speed.

Animation inspector

See how many animations, transitions, videos and canvases a page runs before you capture.

4K & more formats

Export at 3840 px wide, plus PDF, SVG, AVIF, BMP and ICO (Pro).

Private by design

Screenshots and videos are processed and stored only in your browser. Nothing is uploaded.
